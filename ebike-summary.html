Detachable Carriage for E-Bike

Second-Year Mechanical Engineering Project (2024)

With rapid technological advancements, the world is shifting towards cleaner, more affordable, and reusable energy sources. Electric vehicles serve as an excellent alternative to conventional transportation, significantly reducing environmental pollution. Among them, electric bicycles stand out as an energy-efficient and sustainable mode of transport.

The Challenge

In recent years, Nepal has seen a growing interest in the EV market, with electric bicycles gaining popularity. However, most available e-bikes are imported and remain too expensive for the general public. This challenge has inspired our project—the conversion of an existing bicycle into an electric bicycle and the fabrication of a detachable cargo system.

Project Goals

Our goal is to develop an affordable, practical, and efficient transportation solution by retrofitting a conventional bicycle with a battery-powered drive train and integrating a detachable cargo system for added utility. This project offers a cost-effective alternative for daily commuters, small business owners, and delivery services in Nepal.

Impact & Versatility

The detachable cargo enhances the e-bike’s versatility, allowing users to transport goods efficiently while maintaining the bike’s flexibility for personal use. By utilizing locally available resources and minimizing manufacturing costs, we aim to make electric bicycles more accessible, promote sustainable transportation, and reduce dependence on fossil fuels, contributing to a cleaner and greener future for Nepal.
