Algae as a Protein Source for Poultry Feed

Research & Development (Sustainability & Innovation)

A Circular Economy Approach to Wastewater Bioremediation and Low-Cost Algae Utilization using AI-Optimized Algae Cultivation

This project proposes a scalable solution to global aquatic eutrophication and protein feed scarcity by integrating biotechnology with smart engineering. "Green Gold" utilizes algae to bioremediate wastewater, effectively sequestering Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and carbon dioxide. To optimize cultivation dynamics, we employed an IoT monitoring framework (ESP32) and trained a Random Forest Regressor on experimental datasets provided by Assoc. Prof. Paras Mani Timalsina (Kathmandu University).

Methodology & Results
  • AI Optimization: The machine learning model identified chemical-free wastewater as a viable growth medium and pinpointed Day 26 as the critical harvest window to maximize biomass prior to culture collapse.
  • Process Innovation: A novel wet-processing technique was introduced, combining wet biomass with an all-purpose flour binder and utilizing solar drying. This significantly reduces the thermodynamic and financial costs of traditional dehydration.
  • Production: We developed a dual-tier manufacturing approach. This includes a fully automatic palletization system designed via CAD and 3D printing for industrial efficiency, alongside a semi-automatic prototype (wood and 3D-printed components) for local deployment.
  • Viability: Cost analysis indicates a production range of $0.40–0.47 USD/kg without factoring labor cost, significantly below current market rates. The system supports SDGs 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15, demonstrating that responsible production can drive ecological restoration.

The system is designed for maximum flexibility; while automation offers precision, the pelletization process can be successfully replicated manually in resource-constrained settings without compromising product quality. Furthermore, the lifecycle is made 100% circular through pyrolysis: any residual or excess biomass can be converted into bio-oil and bio-char, providing a renewable energy source and a secondary revenue stream.

Ultimately, Green Gold redefines the relationship between ecology and economy, proving that a cleaner planet is the foundation for a wealthier future.
