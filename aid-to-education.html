Aviyantaa x USC & Kathmandu Metropolitan City Initiative

We are thrilled to announce the successful conclusion of the "AID TO EDUCATION" event, a collaborative effort between Aviyantaa x USC and the Kathmandu Metropolitan City. This initiative aimed to empower and uplift the educational landscape in our community, and it's with immense joy that we reflect upon its completion.

Gratitude to Trainers

First and foremost, we extend our heartfelt gratitude to all the trainers who dedicated their time, expertise, and passion to make this event a resounding success. Your commitment to educating and inspiring the next generation is truly commendable, and your impact will be felt for years to come.

Platform Sponsor

We would also like to express our deep appreciation to Shivapuri Secondary School for generously providing the platform for our event. Your support and collaboration have been invaluable, and it has allowed us to reach a wider audience and create a more significant impact.

Resource Allocation

Additionally, we extend our thanks to the individuals and organizations who served as resource allocators, helping to ensure that the event ran smoothly and efficiently. Your contributions were instrumental in making this initiative a reality.

Looking Forward

Thank you once again to all trainers, Shivapuri Secondary School, resource allocators, and everyone who played a part in making "AID TO EDUCATION" a resounding success. Your contributions are deeply appreciated, and we look forward to continuing our mission of educational empowerment in the future.
