Manufacturing Process

Custom Handcrafted Tokens of Love & Case Study

Entrepreneurial Initiative & Lean Manufacturing Research (2025)

Phase 1: Initial Creation & KU Running Shield

As part of the first-ever KU Running Shield Sports Competition, our team designed and manufactured 50 personalized tokens of love to represent and celebrate our university. These tokens were presented to key participants and dignitaries to reflect KU's spirit and innovation.

The craft item (token of love) was initially created by:

  • Mr. Agrim Thapa
  • Mr. Madan Hajam Thakur
  • Mr. Tushar Dhakal

This initiative was carried out under the robotics club of Kathmandu University. Due to the success of the design, these tokens are now utilized by the Technical Training Center (TTC) for manufacturing and fulfilling the university's official needs.

Phase 2: Lean Manufacturing Case Study

Following the adoption of the token design by TTC for continuous production, I collaborated with another team to conduct a comprehensive case study entitled "LEAN MANUFACTURING PRACTICES IN SMALL AND CRAFT INDUSTRIES".

The objective of this research was to analyze the existing production processes and identify how lean manufacturing techniques can be effectively utilized to increase production efficiency and productivity of craft items at the Technical Training Center.

The Team consisted of:

  • Anup Lama
  • Bishal Kumar Silwal
  • Subesh Tharu
  • Sukalpa Das Shrestha
