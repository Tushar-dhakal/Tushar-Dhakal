Pre-event of Aviyantaa ’24

Pre-event of Aviyantaa ’24 organized by the Association of Mechanical Engineering Students Kathmandu University in collaboration with Kathmandu University School of Education and partners including Kathmandu University Robotics Club, SEDS-KU, and Design Lab Kathmandu University.

Managed and coordinated a two-phase technology outreach event conducted in Hattiban on the occasion of Constitution Day.

Phase 1: Training of Trainers (ToT)

Organized a 3-day Training of Trainers (ToT) program for engineering students at Kathmandu University in three technical domains:

  • CAD and 3D Printing
  • Basic Electronics
  • Introductory Space Science

Coordinated expert sessions and logistics for training engineering students so they could later deliver workshops to school students.

Phase 2: Hands-on Technology Workshops

Planned and executed the second phase where trained engineering students conducted hands-on technology workshops for Grades 5–12 students at the School of Education, Hattiban.

Key Responsibilities & Impact
  • Oversaw event planning, institutional coordination, logistics, and program structure, including collaboration with university departments and school partners.
  • Helped facilitate participation of ~200 students from 40+ schools and colleges, promoting early exposure to engineering and technological tools.
  • Coordinated with university leadership and event partners to ensure smooth execution of the program.
