About the SIMM Project

The Strategic Innovation & Manufacturing Management (SIMM) project represents a holistic framework designed to optimize engineering decisions and streamline workflows. From restoring complex CNC systems to designing custom handcrafted products, SIMM leverages quantitative models to evaluate parameters and design optimal solutions.

Lean Manufacturing

Analyzed production cycles for university events, implementing value-stream mapping, bottleneck identification, and cycle-time reduction techniques to save costs and eliminate waste.

AHP Decision Modeling

Deploys mathematical prioritization matrices (specifically Saaty's Analytic Hierarchy Process) to evaluate decisions—like choosing between retrofitting existing systems or investing in new hardware.

AHP Decision Methodology

Saaty's Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) is a structured technique for organizing and analyzing complex decisions.

1. Model the Problem

Identify the overarching goal, define the evaluation criteria, and list the candidate alternatives/options.

2. Pairwise Judgements

Evaluate factors in pairs on a standard scale of 1-9 to extract weights and priorities.

3. Synthesize & Check

Synthesize global scores, analyze inconsistencies, and calculate the Consistency Ratio (CR).
