About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Kathmandu University with strong interests in robotics, energy systems, and applied engineering design. My work focuses on transforming theoretical concepts into practical, real-world solutions through hands-on prototyping, system integration, and experimentation.

I have actively worked on robotic arms, gesture-controlled systems, electric vehicle battery repurposing, and STEAM education initiatives. I am particularly interested in sustainable engineering, automation, and interdisciplinary innovation.

Projects

  • Gesture Controlled Robotic Arm
    A multi-DOF robotic arm controlled using real-time hand gestures through computer vision.
  • Design and Fabrication of 4 D.O.F Robotic Arm
    A servo-driven robotic arm fabricated using 3D-printed PLA components.
  • Energy Economics of EV Battery Repurposing
    Analysis of second-life applications of EV batteries in the context of Nepal.
  • Yantra Kit – STEAM Learning Toolkit
    A low-cost educational kit designed to teach engineering fundamentals to school students.
